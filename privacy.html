Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: Checkset does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone.

What Checkset stores

Your routine, workouts, sets, notes and settings are saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Checkset has no servers. These help and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub, which may log visits under its own privacy policy.

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are on, iOS includes Checkset's data in your iPhone backup, like any app's data. Apple handles those backups under its own terms.

Files you import or export

When you import a CSV file or restore a backup, you choose the file in the Files app. When you export a CSV, make a backup or share a workout card, you choose where it goes in the share sheet (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). Checkset never sends these files anywhere by itself.

Notifications and the Lock Screen

Rest alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing goes over the internet. The alert shows your next exercise and weight. During a workout, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the workout name, your next exercise and weight × reps. iOS shows this on the Lock Screen even when notification previews are hidden. You can turn notifications or Live Activities off for Checkset in the iPhone Settings app.

Photos

Checkset can add your workout card to Photos only when you choose “Save Image” from the share sheet. It cannot see or read your photos.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. They are not designed to contain workout data.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any workout in the app. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone; existing iPhone backups keep a copy until they are replaced.

Children

Checkset collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: joelhcode@gmail.com.
