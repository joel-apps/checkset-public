Checkset is a free workout log: no account, no ads, no subscriptions. Questions not answered here? Email joelhcode@gmail.com.

Getting started

On first launch, choose your units, a split (or build your own) and whether workouts fall on the same days every week or simply follow in order. Weights start blank: enter them in your first workout and keep them as your targets on the Finish screen. Settings is at the bottom of the Routine tab.

Logging a workout

Tap Start workout on Today. Each set is prefilled with your target; tap ✓ when it's done, or use − / + (or tap the number to type) first. “Previous” shows the same set from the last time you did the exercise. Tap Finish when you're done; unchecked sets aren't saved.

Rest timer and alerts

Checking a set starts that exercise's rest. The bar at the bottom has −15 / +15 / Skip; tap the time for 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00. With the app open, a short sound plays and the phone vibrates when rest is over. The sound plays even with the silent switch on; turn it off in Settings if you prefer.

With the phone locked, a rest-over alert arrives at the end time (allow notifications when asked, or later in Settings › Rest alerts when locked). It is marked Time Sensitive so it gets through Focus modes. With the silent switch on, iOS only vibrates. The Lock Screen shows the countdown with −15 / +15 / Skip.

Your routine

The Routine tab lists your workouts in order. Tap one to set its days, its exercises and each exercise's target and rest. “Edit target” during a workout also updates the routine for next time.

Moving from another workout app

Settings › Your data › Import reads CSV exports from Strong and Hevy. In Strong, export your data as CSV from its settings; in Hevy, use its settings to export workouts. Save the file to Files, then pick it in Checkset. Strong files don't say whether weights are kg or lb, so Checkset asks. You can check how each exercise name will be matched before anything is saved, and importing the same file again adds nothing.

Try it with a small sample: sample-workouts.csv.

Backups and export

Settings › Your data › Back up to a file saves one file with everything; Restore replaces what is on the phone with a backup, after checking the whole file. Export as CSV writes your history in a common spreadsheet layout.

Privacy

Checkset collects no data; everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
